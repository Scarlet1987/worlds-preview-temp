How worlds work

  1. World position. After an add, focus lands on the changed section. After a delete, focus lands on what is adjacent to it — because where you start the story is where you are.
  2. Name it yourself. Pressing + at any level prompts you to name that level. The AI never fills it in for you, and each scale you add is the boundary of what can be played in.
  3. Every field is + − your text. Every field carries + to add an instance and − to remove one, with your own text in it. At least one entry is kept.

Nothing hidden. No show-all gates. Labels are always visible; depth comes from scrolling and from these collapsible sections.

Lore Book lives in creator. The full lore book is in Creator's World section. Worlds is the place ladder itself.
